Where this filter is used

Activated carbon filter cartridge is supplied for taste, odour and organic removal in water and process liquid streams. Typical operators include water works, food and beverage plants and chemical process lines.

Elements are matched to the original equipment by dimension, media grade and micron rating, so a replacement can be fitted without modifying the housing or the piping. If you are not sure which element fits your unit, send us the equipment brand, model and a photo of the element in place — we identify it and quote the equivalent.

Manufacturing and delivery

Production runs at our Xinxiang plant in Henan, an ISO9000-certified facility. We have been manufacturing filter elements and cartridges for gas, liquid and oil duty for more than 16 years, and we hold more than 20 patent certificates for filtration structures and media combinations.
