Who we are

Leading provider of custom industrial filtration solutions

XINXIANG DAYU FILTERING EQUIPMENT CO.,LTD is a well-known enterprise that provides filtration solutions. The company is committed to the development, consultation, and service of filtration technology in the field of water treatment, as well as the research and development, production, and sales of filters and filter elements.

Our fully enclosed clean factory building, advanced production equipment and complete production processes — together with an innovative technical and R&D team — ensure the quality of every product that leaves our plant.

Authoritative qualifications

Certified ISO9000 for 9 consecutive years, High-tech Enterprise & IP management system certified.

Standardized clean production

Closed clean workshop, advanced equipment and a professional R&D team guarantee stable product quality.

Full range filter products

Gas filtration R&D
