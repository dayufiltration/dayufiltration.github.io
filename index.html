R&D strength

Eight Years of Focused Filtration R&D

Dedicated research since 2018 — coalescence separation for natural gas pipelines, cyclone filtration, and filter elements for natural gas power plant front-end modules and pressure regulating stations.

Since 2018, our engineering team has focused on gas filtration: coalescence separation filtration for natural gas pipeline transportation, cyclone filtration, and a series of filter elements for the front-end modules and pressure regulating stations of natural gas power plants.

Eight years of dedicated development have produced 10 R&D programs, now protected by 21 utility model patents and 1 invention patent for natural gas filter elements — praised by customers for their scientific value and delivering measurable economic and social benefits.
